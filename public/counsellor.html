Someone told you something, or you saw something? Write it down here. It goes straight to the counsellor. You won't see the case afterwards, and that's fine: it's in safe hands.

How we help

  • Be there, be kind. Being around and friendly stops a lot of trouble before it starts.
  • Don't investigate. Never question people or try to sort out serious problems yourself.
  • Tell straight away. If someone is hurt, threatened or talks about harming themselves, find a teacher now and pass it on here.
  • Keep it private. Don't share what people tell you with friends.
  • Point to SafeVoice. Anyone can report anonymously from their phone.

Roles

  • Administrator Everything below, plus team, audit log, settings and data.
  • Counsellor Reads and answers cases, posts notices, sees insights.
  • Principal (insights only) Trends, hotspots, response times and outcomes. Never sees what a student wrote.
  • Peer supporter (student) Gets tasks from the counsellor and passes on concerns. Never sees any report.

Built to protect students

  • Anonymous by design. No names. No IP addresses. No login for students.
  • Need-to-know access. Leadership sees trends, never stories. Every case view is logged.
  • Human in the loop. AI prioritises. A counsellor decides.
  • Deleted on schedule. Reports are not kept forever.
